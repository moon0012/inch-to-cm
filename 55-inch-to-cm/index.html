📐 Where Is 55 Inches (139.7 cm) Used?

55 inches is one of the most commercially important measurements, especially in the TV industry. Here's where it matters:

📺 Television Screens

The 55-inch TV is the world's most popular TV size as of 2026. It hits the sweet spot between screen immersion and room compatibility:

  • Large enough for a cinematic feel in standard living rooms
  • Small enough to not overwhelm most wall spaces
  • Available from every major brand (Samsung, LG, Sony, TCL, Hisense)
  • Offered in all display technologies: LED, QLED, OLED, Mini-LED

❓ Frequently Asked Questions About 55 Inches

What is 55 inches in cm?
55 inches equals 139.70 centimeters. To convert: 55 × 2.54 = 139.70 cm. This is also 1,397 mm or 1.397 meters, roughly 4 feet 7 inches.
What are the exact dimensions of a 55-inch TV?
A 55-inch TV with 16:9 aspect ratio measures: 139.7 cm diagonal, approximately 121.6 cm wide, and 68.2 cm tall. With the bezel, add about 2–3 cm on each side. See our 55-inch TV dimensions page for more details.
55 or 65 inch TV — which is better?
It depends on room size and viewing distance. The 55" TV is ideal for standard living rooms (12–20 m²) at 2–3m distance. The 65" TV has 40% more screen area and suits larger rooms (15+ m²) at 3+ meters. If budget allows and space permits, bigger is usually better for 4K content. Read our full comparison →
How far should I sit from a 55-inch TV?
For 4K content: 1.7–2.8 meters (sweet spot: ~2.1m). For 1080p content: 2.1–3.4 meters. The Society of Motion Picture & Television Engineers (SMPTE) recommends a 30° viewing angle for an immersive experience, which translates to about 2.1 meters for a 55" TV.
Is a 55-inch TV too big for a bedroom?
It depends on the bedroom size. For small bedrooms (8–10 m²) where you sit 1.5m away, yes — a 43-inch or 50-inch TV is better. For larger master bedrooms (12+ m²) with 2+ meter viewing distance, a 55-inch TV works beautifully.
Why is 55 inches the most popular TV size?
55 inches became the #1 selling TV size because it perfectly balances immersion with practicality. At ~121.6 cm wide, it fits on most TV stands and wall spaces. The price point is also sweet: $350–600 for solid 4K models. It's the "Goldilocks" size for standard 12–18 m² living rooms with 2–3m viewing distance.
