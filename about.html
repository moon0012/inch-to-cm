About Us

The people behind your go-to measurement conversion tool

Who We Are

inch-to-cm.online was founded by a small team of developers and measurement enthusiasts who noticed a surprisingly common frustration: quickly and reliably converting between inches and centimeters. Whether you are shopping for furniture from an overseas retailer, following a recipe written in unfamiliar units, or sizing a monitor for your desk, you deserve a tool that gives you an accurate answer in seconds, not one buried under ads and pop-ups.

We are based across multiple time zones and share a background in web engineering, data accuracy, and user experience design. That combination drives everything we build: fast load times, clean interfaces, and results you can trust.

Our Mission

Our mission is straightforward: make metric and imperial conversion accessible to every person on the planet, regardless of technical skill or the device they happen to be using. The world still runs on two measurement systems, and the gap between them should never slow you down.

We believe a conversion tool should load instantly, produce correct results every single time, and stay out of your way. No sign-up walls, no unnecessary complexity, no hidden costs. You type a number, you get an answer. That is the experience we protect.

Why We Built This Tool

The idea came from a real moment of frustration. One of our founders was ordering custom shelving from a manufacturer that listed dimensions in inches, while the room had been measured in centimeters. The existing online converters were either cluttered with intrusive advertising, slow to load on mobile, or surprisingly inaccurate when handling decimal values.

We decided to build something better: a single-purpose tool that does one thing exceptionally well. The conversion factor between inches and centimeters (1 inch = 2.54 cm) is defined by international standard, and our tool implements that standard with no rounding shortcuts. The result is a converter you can rely on for everything from casual estimates to professional-grade precision.
