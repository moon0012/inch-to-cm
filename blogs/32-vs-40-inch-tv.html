When to Choose 32 Inches

  • Desktop monitor replacement
  • Kitchen or bathroom TV
  • Viewing distance under 1.5m
  • Budget under $200

When to Choose 40 Inches

  • Bedroom primary TV
  • Small apartment living room
  • Viewing distance 1.5–2.5m
  • Casual gaming

📌 Our Pick

For most people, the 40-inch TV is the better buy. It's 56% more screen area for only a small price increase. The 32" is only better if you're putting it on a desk or have very limited space.
