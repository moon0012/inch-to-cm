🏆 Which Size Should You Choose?

Choose 32" if:

  • Your room is small (under 10 m²)
  • You sit 1.0–1.5 meters from the screen
  • It's for a kitchen, guest room, or secondary bedroom
  • Budget is under $200
  • You need a computer monitor-sized TV

Choose 43" if:

  • You have a small living room or standard bedroom
  • Viewing distance is 1.5–2.5 meters
  • You want 4K at the best price-per-inch
  • Room is 10–15 m²
  • You want a "just right" size that doesn't dominate the room

Choose 55" if:

  • You have a standard or large living room (12+ m²)
  • Viewing distance is 2.0–3.0 meters
  • You want an immersive movie and gaming experience
  • You're willing to invest in a quality panel (OLED, QLED)
  • It's your primary entertainment screen

💡 Key Takeaways

  • The jump from 32" to 43" gives you 80% more screen area — the biggest proportional upgrade
  • Going from 43" to 55" adds another 64% screen area and unlocks premium panel options
  • A 55" TV has nearly 3× the screen area of a 32" — they're completely different experiences
  • Most 43" and 55" TVs in 2026 are 4K; most 32" TVs are still 1080p
  • When in doubt, go one size up — bigger almost always wins with modern 4K resolution
