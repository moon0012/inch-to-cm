The 65-inch TV has roughly 40% more screen area than the 55-inch — a significant upgrade.

🛋️ When to Choose 55 Inches

  • Your viewing distance is 2–2.5 meters
  • Wall space is under 130 cm wide
  • You want to save $200–$400 on average
  • The TV is for a bedroom or secondary room

🛋️ When to Choose 65 Inches

  • Your viewing distance is 2.5–4 meters
  • You have at least 150 cm of wall space
  • It's your main living room TV
  • You watch sports, movies, or play games frequently

💰 Price Difference in 2026

The price gap between 55" and 65" TVs has narrowed significantly. For popular brands, expect to pay $150–$400 more for the 65-inch version. Given the 40% screen area increase, the 65" is often the better value.

📌 Our Verdict

If you can fit it and afford it, go 65 inches. The size upgrade is substantial, and the price difference keeps shrinking. Only choose 55" if your room is genuinely too small or you're mounting it in a bedroom.
