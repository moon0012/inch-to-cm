🧬 What Determines Height?

  • Genetics (60–80%) — The most significant factor; height tends to run in families
  • Nutrition — Adequate protein, calcium, and vitamins during childhood are critical
  • Healthcare — Access to medical care during growth years affects final height
  • Economic development — Wealthier nations tend to have taller populations
  • Sleep — Growth hormone is primarily released during deep sleep
