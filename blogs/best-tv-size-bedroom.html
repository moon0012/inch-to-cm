🛏️ Detailed Breakdown by Size

32-Inch TV (81.28 cm) — The Compact Choice

A 32-inch TV measures approximately 70.7 cm wide × 39.7 cm tall. It's perfect for:

  • Small bedrooms, guest rooms, and dorm rooms
  • Mounting on a wall above a dresser
  • Placing on a nightstand or small table
  • Viewing distance: 1.0–1.5 meters

Best for: When space is tight or the TV is a secondary screen. Most 32" TVs in 2026 are 720p or 1080p — perfectly fine at this size.

40–43 Inch TV (101.6–109.2 cm) — The Sweet Spot

A 43-inch TV (109.2 cm diagonal, ~95 cm wide) is our top recommendation for most bedrooms:

  • Large enough to enjoy movies and shows from bed
  • Small enough to not overwhelm the room
  • Widely available in 4K at affordable prices ($200–$400)
  • Viewing distance: 1.5–2.5 meters (perfect bed-to-wall distance)

Why 43" wins: The typical bedroom has 2–3 meters from bed to the opposite wall. At 2 meters, a 43" 4K TV delivers crisp, immersive visuals without eyestrain.

50 Inch TV (127.0 cm) — Going Bigger

A 50-inch TV (127 cm diagonal, ~110.5 cm wide) works when:

  • Your bedroom is 14+ m² with 2.5+ meter viewing distance
  • You prioritize a cinematic experience over a cozy vibe
  • You plan to wall-mount (keeps floor space clear)

55 Inch TV (139.7 cm) — Master Bedroom Only

A 55-inch TV in a bedroom is a luxury, but it works in spacious master bedrooms:

  • Minimum room: 16 m² with 2.5+ meter viewing distance
  • Wall mounting is almost mandatory at this size
  • Consider an OLED for thinner profile and better dark-room performance

📏 How to Measure Your Bedroom for a TV

  1. Measure viewing distance: From where your head is on the pillow to the opposite wall
  2. Measure wall width: Where the TV will go — leave at least 15 cm on each side
  3. Check height: Center of screen should be at eye level when sitting up in bed (~1.0–1.2 m from floor)
  4. Use our calculator: Visit the homepage to use our room size recommender tool

🌙 Bedroom-Specific Tips

  • Blue light: Enable night mode/warm colors for evening viewing
  • Sound: Consider a small soundbar — bedroom TVs often face the bed at an angle
  • Smart features: Sleep timer is essential — set it before dozing off
  • OLED advantage: Perfect blacks mean less ambient light in dark bedrooms
  • Mounting height: Slightly higher than living room TVs to account for bed position

❓ FAQ

Is a 32-inch TV big enough for a bedroom?
For small bedrooms (under 10 m²) with 1–1.5m viewing distance, yes. But most people today prefer at least 40–43 inches for their bedroom. TVs have gotten cheaper, and a 43-inch 4K TV can be found for under $250.
Can I put a 55-inch TV in my bedroom?
Yes, if your bedroom is 16+ m² and you sit at least 2.5 meters from the screen. Wall mounting is recommended. A 55-inch TV (139.7 cm diagonal) provides an excellent experience in large master bedrooms.
What about mounting height in a bedroom?
Mount the TV so the center of the screen is at eye level when sitting up in bed — typically 100–120 cm from the floor. This is slightly higher than living room mounting (90–100 cm) because you're viewing from an elevated position.
