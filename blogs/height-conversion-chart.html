📏 How to Convert Height to Centimeters

Converting a height like 5'10" to centimeters is a three-step process:

  1. Convert feet to inches: Multiply the feet by 12. For 5 feet: 5 × 12 = 60 inches.
  2. Add remaining inches: 60 + 10 = 70 inches total.
  3. Multiply by 2.54: 70 × 2.54 = 177.80 cm.

That's it! The formula works for any height. Let's look at the full chart.

💡 Quick Height Conversion Tips

Memorize these benchmark heights for quick mental math:

  • 5'0" = 152 cm (the baseline — add ~2.5 cm per inch from here)
  • 5'6" = 168 cm
  • 6'0" = 183 cm
  • Each inch = 2.54 cm
  • Each foot = 30.48 cm

For any height, start from the nearest benchmark and add or subtract 2.5 cm per inch. It's fast and accurate within 1 cm.
