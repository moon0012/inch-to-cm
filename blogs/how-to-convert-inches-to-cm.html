How to Convert Inches to Centimeters

The simple formula with practical examples

📐 The Formula

centimeters = inches × 2.54

One inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters. This is an exact definition established by international agreement in 1959.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: 10 inches to cm

10 × 2.54 = 25.4 cm

Example 2: 32 inches to cm

32 × 2.54 = 81.28 cm

Example 3: 65 inches to cm

65 × 2.54 = 165.1 cm

🔄 Converting CM to Inches

To go the other way: inches = centimeters ÷ 2.54

Example: 100 cm ÷ 2.54 = 39.37 inches
