How to Measure TV Size Correctly

A simple step-by-step guide to understanding TV dimensions

📏 Step 1: Understand Diagonal Measurement

TV sizes are always measured diagonally — from one corner of the screen to the opposite corner. A "55-inch TV" means the diagonal measures 55 inches (139.7 cm).

Important: This measures the visible screen only, not the frame or bezel.

📐 Step 2: Measure Your TV

  1. Use a tape measure or measuring tape
  2. Place one end at the bottom-left corner of the screen (not the frame)
  3. Stretch diagonally to the top-right corner
  4. Read the measurement — that's your TV size

📊 Step 3: Convert to Centimeters

If your tape measure shows inches, multiply by 2.54 to get centimeters. Or use our inch to cm converter.

🔢 Step 4: Find Width and Height

For a standard 16:9 TV:

  • Width = diagonal × 0.87
  • Height = diagonal × 0.49

For example, a 65-inch TV: width ≈ 56.5" (~143.5 cm), height ≈ 31.8" (~80.8 cm).

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Measuring width instead of diagonal
  • Including the bezel in your measurement
  • Measuring the shipping box instead of the screen
  • Confusing screen size with total TV dimensions (including stand)
