How to Measure TV Size Correctly

A comprehensive step-by-step guide to understanding and measuring TV dimensions

📏 Step 1: Understand Diagonal Measurement

TV sizes are always measured diagonally — from one corner of the screen to the opposite corner. When someone says they have a "55-inch TV," they mean the diagonal measures 55 inches (139.7 cm).

This convention dates back to the early days of cathode ray tube (CRT) televisions, when the round picture tube was naturally described by its diameter. Even though modern TVs are flat rectangles, the diagonal measurement tradition stuck.

Key point: The advertised size measures the visible screen only, not the outer frame (bezel). However, some manufacturers may include the bezel in the measurement, so it's always worth measuring yourself.

📐 Step 2: Measure Your TV Screen

Here's how to accurately measure your TV:

  1. Get a tape measure — a flexible fabric or retractable metal tape measure works best. Avoid rulers as they're too short.
  2. Find the bottom-left corner of the visible screen area (where the picture displays, not the frame).
  3. Stretch diagonally to the top-right corner of the visible screen area.
  4. Read the measurement — this number is your TV's screen size.
  5. Round to the nearest standard size: TVs come in standard sizes (32", 40", 43", 50", 55", 65", 75", 85"), so your measurement should be very close to one of these.

If your tape measure shows centimeters, divide by 2.54 to get the inch size. Or simply use our converter.

🔢 Step 4: Calculate Width and Height

The diagonal alone doesn't tell you how much wall space you need. To find the actual width and height of a 16:9 TV (the standard aspect ratio), use these formulas:

  • Width = diagonal × 0.87 (inches) or diagonal × 2.21 (cm from inches)
  • Height = diagonal × 0.49 (inches) or diagonal × 1.24 (cm from inches)

Example for a 65-inch TV:

  • Width = 65 × 0.87 = 56.6 inches ≈ 143.7 cm
  • Height = 65 × 0.49 = 31.9 inches ≈ 80.6 cm

This means you need at least 153 cm of horizontal wall space (adding ~10 cm for the frame/bezel) to mount a 65-inch TV.

📏 Step 5: Measure Total Dimensions (Including Stand)

If you're placing your TV on a table or entertainment center rather than wall-mounting, you also need to know the dimensions with the stand:

  • Width with stand: Usually the same as the TV width, but some stands have wide feet that extend further
  • Height with stand: Typically adds 5–10 cm to the screen height
  • Depth with stand: Usually 18–30 cm depending on the stand design
  • Stand width: Some stands are narrow (fits on smaller furniture), while others are wide (may need a large TV table)

Check the manufacturer's specifications for exact stand dimensions, as these vary significantly between brands and models.

⚠️ Common Measurement Mistakes

Avoid these frequent errors when measuring TV size:

  • Measuring width instead of diagonal: The most common mistake. A TV's width is always smaller than its advertised diagonal size. A 55" TV is only ~48" wide.
  • Including the bezel/frame: Measure only the visible screen area, not the plastic frame around it. Modern TVs have thin bezels, so the difference is small but measurable.
  • Measuring the shipping box: Box dimensions are much larger than the TV itself. Always measure the actual screen.
  • Confusing screen size with total TV size: The total TV (including stand) can be significantly larger than the screen alone. A 65" TV with a stand might be 90 cm tall, even though the screen is only 80.6 cm.
  • Measuring at an angle: Make sure your tape measure runs straight from corner to corner. A slight curve will give an inaccurate reading.

🛋️ How to Choose the Right TV Size

Once you know your TV size, the next question is whether it's right for your room. Here are quick guidelines:

  • Multiply your viewing distance (in cm) by 0.625 to get the ideal TV diagonal in cm
  • Under 2 meters away: 32–43 inch TV
  • 2–3 meters away: 50–55 inch TV
  • 3–4 meters away: 65–75 inch TV
  • 4+ meters away: 77–85 inch TV
