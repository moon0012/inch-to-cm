🤔 Is 180 cm a "Good" Height?

Height perception varies by culture, but 180 cm is widely considered a desirable height for men:

  • It's tall enough to stand out in a crowd without being "too tall"
  • Clothing fits well — standard sizes work for most 180 cm individuals
  • In many countries, it's the "ideal" height listed in surveys and dating preferences
  • For women at 180 cm, you're in the top 1% — similar to many professional models and athletes
