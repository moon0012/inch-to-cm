🛋️ How to Choose by Room Size

The most common mistake is buying a TV that's too small. With 4K resolution, you can sit closer without seeing pixels, so bigger is almost always better.

Small Room (Under 10 m²)

Go with a 32-inch or 43-inch TV. At 1.5–2 meters viewing distance, a 43" delivers a great experience.

Medium Room (10–20 m²)

A 50-inch or 55-inch TV is the sweet spot. The 55" has become the most popular TV size globally — it's large enough to be immersive without overwhelming the space.

Large Room (20+ m²)

Don't go below 65 inches. A 75-inch or even 85-inch TV creates a true cinema experience at 3–5 meters viewing distance.

📐 The Viewing Distance Formula

For 4K TVs: Screen diagonal × 1.5 = minimum viewing distance

For example, a 55-inch TV (139.7 cm) works best at ~2.1 meters minimum.

💡 Pro Tips for 2026

  • Always measure your wall space before buying — check our TV size conversion chart for exact dimensions
  • Account for bezels — add 2–5 cm to each side
  • Consider a TV mount to save floor space
  • 4K resolution means you can sit closer without seeing pixels
  • OLED TVs have thinner profiles — great for wall mounting
  • Most 2026 TVs include built-in smart features, so skip the external streaming device
