TV Size vs Viewing Distance: Complete Guide (2026)

The definitive chart — find the ideal TV size for any seating distance

The most common mistake people make when buying a TV is choosing one that's too small. With modern 4K and 8K resolution, you can sit much closer than with older TVs. This guide gives you the exact formula and a complete reference chart.

📐 The Simple Formula

For 4K TVs: Viewing Distance ÷ 1.5 = Ideal Screen Diagonal

Example: 3 meters ÷ 1.5 = 2 meters (200 cm) diagonal → 75-inch TV

This formula is based on recommendations from the Society of Motion Picture & Television Engineers (SMPTE), which suggests a 30° viewing angle for an immersive experience.

📺 Resolution Matters More Than You Think

The reason 4K TVs changed the viewing distance game:

  • 720p HD: Pixels visible up close — sit far away
  • 1080p Full HD: Good clarity at moderate distances
  • 4K Ultra HD: Can sit 50% closer than 1080p without seeing pixels
  • 8K: Can sit even closer, but very few 8K content sources exist in 2026

Bottom line: with a 4K TV, don't be afraid to go big. A 65-inch 4K TV at 2.5 meters looks incredible.

💡 Pro Tips

  • When in doubt, go one size bigger — you won't regret it with 4K
  • Measure from your eyes (seated position) to where the TV will be
  • Account for the TV stand or mount depth
  • If you frequently switch between sitting positions, choose based on the farthest distance
  • Use painter's tape on the wall to visualize the TV size before buying
