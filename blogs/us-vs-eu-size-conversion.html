📏 How to Measure Yourself

  1. Bust/Chest: Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it level
  2. Waist: Measure around your natural waistline (narrowest point, usually above the navel)
  3. Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips/buttocks
  4. Inseam: From crotch to ankle bone
  5. Foot: Stand on paper, trace your foot, measure the longest point

Pro tip: Always measure in centimeters — it's more precise than inches for clothing.

💡 Shopping Tips

  • EU sizes run slightly smaller than US sizes in most brands
  • Asian sizes (Japan, Korea) tend to run 1–2 sizes smaller than US
  • Always check the brand's specific size chart — standards vary wildly
  • When between sizes, order the larger size — you can alter down easier
  • For online shopping, know your measurements in cm — they're universal
