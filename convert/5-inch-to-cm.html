5 Inches to Centimeters

5 inches = 12.70 cm
Formula: 5 × 2.54 = 12.70 cm

Where 5 Inches Shows Up in Real Life

Five inches, or 12.70 centimeters, is a measurement deeply embedded in the world of smartphones and portable technology. For years, a 5-inch display was considered the standard smartphone screen size, offering a balance between usability and portability. Although modern phones have grown larger, many users still search for compact devices in this range, especially across Asian and European markets where smaller handsets remain popular.

In photography, 5 inches is the short side of the popular 5x7 print format, widely used for framed portraits and greeting cards. When ordering prints from a photo lab, understanding that 5 inches converts to 12.70 cm helps international customers select the correct format. Craft and scrapbooking enthusiasts also work frequently at this scale, cutting cardstock and embellishments to 5-inch dimensions for standard page layouts.

The 5-inch measurement is also significant in cooking. Many ramekins and small baking dishes have a diameter close to 5 inches, making this conversion useful when following international recipes that specify dish sizes in centimeters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many centimeters is 5 inches?
5 inches equals 12.70 centimeters. You can calculate this by multiplying 5 by the conversion factor 2.54.
Is a 5-inch phone screen big or small?
By today's standards, a 5-inch (12.70 cm) screen is considered compact. Most modern smartphones range from 6 to 6.7 inches. However, 5-inch phones are preferred by users who want a device that fits comfortably in one hand or a pocket.
What photo print sizes are close to 5 inches?
The 5x7 inch format uses 5 inches (12.70 cm) as its short dimension and is one of the most popular print sizes for framed photos. The classic 4x6 print is slightly smaller, with the long side at 6 inches (15.24 cm).
