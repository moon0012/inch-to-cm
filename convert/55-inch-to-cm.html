55 Inches to Centimeters

55 inches = 139.70 cm
Formula: 55 × 2.54 = 139.70 cm

Why 55 Inches Dominates the TV Market

Fifty-five inches equals 139.70 centimeters, and this measurement has become synonymous with one product above all others: the living room television. The 55-inch TV is consistently the best-selling size category globally, outselling both smaller and larger models. Its popularity stems from hitting the perfect balance between immersive viewing and practical room fit.

For room planning, 55 inches (139.70 cm) is remarkably close to the average adult arm span, which provides an intuitive way to estimate the TV's diagonal. If you stretch your arms wide, the distance from fingertip to fingertip approximates this screen size. The actual screen dimensions of a 55-inch 16:9 TV are approximately 48 inches (121.9 cm) wide by 27 inches (68.6 cm) tall, important measurements for ensuring it fits on your TV stand or wall mount bracket.

When purchasing televisions from international retailers, the same TV may be marketed as a "140 cm class" model in Europe and Asia. Samsung, LG, and Sony all use centimeter designations in their metric-market product listings. Understanding that 55 inches is 139.70 cm helps shoppers cross-reference models and pricing across regions. For optimal 4K viewing, industry experts recommend sitting 4.5 to 7 feet (1.4 to 2.1 meters) from a 55-inch screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many centimeters is 55 inches?
55 inches equals 139.70 centimeters, or approximately 1.40 meters. This is the diagonal screen measurement of the world's most popular TV size.
What is the ideal viewing distance for a 55-inch TV?
For a 55-inch (139.70 cm) 4K TV, the recommended viewing distance is 4.5 to 7 feet (1.4 to 2.1 meters). For 1080p content, sitting 7 to 9 feet (2.1 to 2.7 meters) away provides a better experience where individual pixels are not visible.
What are the actual width and height of a 55-inch TV?
A 55-inch TV with a 16:9 aspect ratio measures approximately 48 inches (121.9 cm) wide and 27 inches (68.6 cm) tall for the screen panel alone. Including bezels and a stand, the total dimensions will be slightly larger, so always check manufacturer specs before buying furniture.
