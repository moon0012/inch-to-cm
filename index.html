❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert inches to centimeters?
Multiply the inch value by 2.54. For example, 32 inches × 2.54 = 81.28 cm. You can use our converter above for instant results, or browse our complete conversion chart.
What size is a 55-inch TV in centimeters?
A 55-inch TV measures 139.7 cm diagonally. The screen is approximately 121.6 cm wide and 68.2 cm tall. It's the most popular TV size for medium-sized living rooms. See our full TV size chart.
How tall is 6 feet in cm?
6 feet equals 72 inches or 182.88 cm. This is considered tall in most countries. Check our height conversion chart for more heights.
What is waist size 32 in cm?
A 32-inch waist equals 81.28 cm. This is a medium size in most clothing brands (US size 10, EU size 42). See our clothing size conversion guide for more sizes.
What is the formula for cm to inches?
Divide the centimeter value by 2.54. For example, 100 cm ÷ 2.54 = 39.37 inches. Or use our converter above — just enter a value in the CM field.

About Inch to CM Conversion

Inches and centimeters are two of the most commonly used units of length measurement worldwide. The inch (in or ") is used primarily in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada, while the centimeter (cm) is used in the metric system adopted by most other countries.

Our site provides instant, accurate conversion between inches and centimeters, plus specialized guides for the most common use cases:

  • TV & Screen Sizes — Find the exact dimensions of any TV in centimeters
  • Height Conversion — Convert feet & inches to cm for height measurements
  • Clothing Sizes — Waist, chest, and hip measurements in cm
  • Conversion Chart — Complete 1-100 inch reference table

The conversion factor is exact: 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters, established by international agreement in 1959. All conversions on this site use this precise standard.
