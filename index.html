Understanding Inches and Centimeters

The inch and the centimeter are two of the most commonly used units of length in everyday life. While they measure the same thing — distance — they belong to different measurement systems and are used in different parts of the world. Understanding both units and how to convert between them is essential for international shopping, travel, construction, and science.

What Is an Inch?

The inch (symbol: in or ″) is a unit of length in the Imperial and US customary measurement systems. It is defined as exactly 25.4 millimeters, or 2.54 centimeters. The inch was historically based on the width of a human thumb. Today, it is primarily used in the United States, Canada (informally), and the United Kingdom for everyday measurements such as screen sizes, paper dimensions, and body measurements.

What Is a Centimeter?

The centimeter (symbol: cm) is a unit of length in the metric system, equal to one hundredth of a meter. The metric system was developed in France during the 1790s and has since been adopted by almost every country in the world. Centimeters are widely used for body measurements, clothing sizes, screen dimensions in many markets, and scientific research. One centimeter is approximately the width of a standard pencil.

The Conversion Formula

The relationship between inches and centimeters is exact and straightforward:

  • Inches to Centimeters: Multiply inches by 2.54 → cm = inches × 2.54
  • Centimeters to Inches: Divide centimeters by 2.54 → inches = cm ÷ 2.54

This conversion factor was internationally agreed upon in 1959 as part of the international yard and pound agreement. It means that 1 inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters — not an approximation, but a precise definition. This makes conversions between inches and centimeters among the most accurate unit conversions you can perform.

Why Do We Need to Convert Between Inches and CM?

Despite efforts to standardize measurements globally, the imperial and metric systems continue to coexist. Americans measure TV screens in inches, while Europeans list them in centimeters. Clothing sold in the US uses inches for waist and inseam, while international brands use centimeters. Construction materials, furniture dimensions, and even recipe measurements can vary by region. Being able to quickly and accurately convert between these units saves time, prevents costly mistakes, and helps you make informed purchasing decisions — whether you are buying a sofa online from another country or following an international sewing pattern.

🚀

👍

The word "inch" comes from the Latin "uncia" meaning "one-twelfth" (of a foot). Historically, it was defined as the width of a man's thumb, then later as the length of three barleycorns placed end to end.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula for converting inches to centimeters?
To convert inches to centimeters, multiply the inch value by 2.54. The formula is: cm = inches × 2.54. For example, 10 inches × 2.54 = 25.4 cm. This conversion factor is exact and internationally standardized.
How many centimeters are in 1 inch?
1 inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters. This definition was established in 1959 through the international yard and pound agreement and is recognized worldwide. It is not an approximation — it is a precise, defined relationship.
How do I convert my height from feet and inches to centimeters?
First, convert your height entirely to inches by multiplying the feet by 12 and adding the remaining inches. Then multiply the total inches by 2.54. For example, if you are 5'10": 5 × 12 + 10 = 70 inches. 70 × 2.54 = 177.8 cm. Read our full height conversion guide for more examples.
Why do some countries use inches while others use centimeters?
The United States, Liberia, and Myanmar primarily use the imperial system (inches, feet, yards), while nearly every other country uses the metric system (centimeters, meters). The metric system was developed in France in the 1790s for its simplicity (based on powers of 10). The US retained imperial units due to the economic cost of switching and deep cultural familiarity. Learn more in our Metric vs Imperial guide.
How do I convert centimeters to inches?
To convert centimeters to inches, divide the centimeter value by 2.54. The formula is: inches = cm ÷ 2.54. For example, 30 cm ÷ 2.54 = 11.81 inches. You can also use our converter above — just enter the value in the "Centimeters to Inches" panel.
What TV sizes look like in centimeters?
TV sizes are measured diagonally. Common conversions: 32" = 81.3 cm, 43" = 109.2 cm, 50" = 127 cm, 55" = 139.7 cm, 65" = 165.1 cm, 75" = 190.5 cm, 85" = 215.9 cm. Note that these are diagonal measurements — the actual width and height are smaller. See our complete TV size guide.
Is this converter accurate enough for professional use?
Yes. This converter uses the exact, internationally standardized conversion factor of 2.54 cm per inch (as defined by ISO 31). All calculations are performed to two decimal places. This level of precision is suitable for engineering, construction, scientific research, and manufacturing applications.
Can I convert decimal and fractional inches?
Yes, our converter handles decimal values with full precision. Enter values like 5.5, 12.25, or 0.75 directly. For fractional inches (like 3/8"), convert the fraction to a decimal first: 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375, then enter 0.375. Common fractions: 1/4" = 0.25, 1/2" = 0.5, 3/4" = 0.75, 1/8" = 0.125, 3/8" = 0.375, 5/8" = 0.625, 7/8" = 0.875.
